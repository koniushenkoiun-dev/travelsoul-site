Where can I use TravelSoul?

Is it free to download?

Yes. Plan and keep one trip at a time for free. Multiple saved trips require Pro.

What does Pro add?

Walking tours, place guides, food picks with Google ratings and Trip Assistant.

What is a Trip Pass?

A one-time unlock for one city’s paid features. Pro covers all cities and is required for multiple saved trips.
